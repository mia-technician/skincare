* How to read this table:

  • ⭐ Tolerability — higher = gentler.
  • 🔥 Strength — higher = stronger.
  • 💧 Dry Skin Fit — higher = better for dry skin (richer textures, stronger buffers).
  • 🌿 Oily Skin Fit — higher = better for oily / acne-prone skin (lighter textures, less residue).
  • 🧴 Follicle-Friendly — higher = less likely to clog pores / trigger closed comedones. Based on known comedogenic ingredients and official "non-comedogenic" claims, not brand rankings.

  • Melasma and rosacea should never be managed based on "which retinol is gentlest" alone — always assess in person.
